Day 41 · Millbrook, Pell County

Dead Hours

I was stuck in traffic at the crossroads in Millbrook the morning it reached town. I've been shut in the storeroom at the gas station ever since. It's October 29th, day 41, the food ran out yesterday, and the town is full of people who died here and didn't stay down.

My pickup is still out at the crossroads, but somebody siphoned it dry. Three cans of gas would get me out: one in the shed behind the last house on the west road, one behind the clinic, one by the wreck on the east road, still burning from when somebody tried to run for it this morning.

How it started

Sept 2
Deer along Cole Creek stop running from people. They walk in slow circles, thin and drooling. The game warden calls it wasting disease, except it kills in days instead of years.
Sept 13
The state lab says to burn the carcasses. The county won't pay for an incinerator, so a road crew buries 300 of them in a trench by the creek, upstream of the town reservoir.
Sept 19
Dale Pruitt, a hunter bitten by a buck he had already shot, dies of fever at Millbrook Clinic. Forty minutes later he gets up off the gurney and bites the night nurse.
Oct 3
Buses take 112 people to the Camp Whitlock relief center. Someone hides a bite. The camp falls overnight and the buses turn back.
Oct 9
The state seals three counties as Containment Zone 4. The weather radio has repeated the same message ever since.

The clock doesn't stop. The sun sets a little before seven, and in daylight they can see you from across a field; in the dark they find you by sound. You'll need to eat, drink, and sleep. Sleep somewhere you can shut a door: the truck cab, or the storeroom at the gas station.

Millbrook is only the middle of it. The whole county inside the containment fence is open: farms, a country church, the relief camp, the reservoir, the quarry, miles of jammed interstate, and whatever is still walking around out there. Press M for the map; places go on it once you've found them.

The rest is still out there: a warden's log, the clinic's records, a lab report nobody wanted to read. Pick up papers with E and reread them in your journal with J.
